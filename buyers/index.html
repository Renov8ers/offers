How it works

What do I get?

An email when a property is under contract and ready to assign: the address, photos, comparable public sales, a value range and our assignment price.

How do I buy one?

Open the deal, claim it and sign the assignment online. The closing runs through an investor-friendly title company or real estate attorney.

Who are you?

Renov8ers LLC is a Long Island real estate investment company. We buy for our own account and may assign our contract to another buyer. We're not a licensed broker or agent.

Will you share my email?

No. It's used only to send you deals, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
